FOREX SCAMS

Have you fallen victim to unscrupulous individuals running Forex scams? We specialize in recovering funds lost to fraudulent Forex trading platforms and brokers. Our expert team uses legal procedures and financial sector expertise to confront these entities and recover your money.

STOCK TRADING SCAMS

Many investors have fallen for empty, deceitful promises in stock trading. If you've lost money to fraudulent stock brokers, pump-and-dump schemes, or fake investment platforms, we can help you recover your funds through systematic legal recovery processes.

CRYPTO FRAUD

The cryptocurrency market's relative lack of regulation has made it a playground for con artists. From fake exchanges to exit scams and Ponzi schemes, we help victims of crypto fraud get back what they lost. Our team understands the complexities of blockchain transactions and can trace funds effectively.

BINARY OPTIONS SCAMS

One of the most common financial scams since 2021 and one we have helped countless victims recover from. Binary options fraud often involves manipulated platforms, false promises, and unauthorized withdrawals. We systematically confront these entities and recover your lost funds.

NIGERIAN SCAMS

Traditionally this type of scam starts with an unsolicited email or message proclaiming to be from a wealthy individual, prince, or business opportunity. If you've sent money in response to these schemes, we can help trace the transactions and potentially recover funds through legal channels.

PONZI SCHEMES

Ponzi schemes promise high returns with little risk, using new investors' money to pay earlier investors. When these schemes collapse, victims lose everything. We help identify the entities involved and pursue legal action to recover your investments.

MT760/MT799 FRAUD

Bank guarantee and standby letter of credit frauds (MT760/MT799) are sophisticated schemes that target businesses and investors. These documents are often forged or manipulated. Our team specializes in exposing these frauds and recovering funds for victims.

ONLINE INVESTMENT SCAMS

From fake trading platforms to fraudulent investment opportunities advertised online, we help recover funds lost to various online investment scams. Our systematic approach includes gathering evidence, confronting entities, and pursuing legal recovery.
