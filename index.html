Frequently Asked Questions

What is "No Win, No Fee"? +
"No Win, No Fee" means you pay absolutely nothing if we don't successfully recover your assets. Our fee is only a percentage of what we recover for you. The initial assessment is 100% free with no obligation.
How long does asset recovery take? +
Recovery times vary based on asset type and complexity. Simple cases can be resolved in 30-45 days, while complex cases may take 3-6 months. We provide a clear timeline after the free assessment.
What types of assets can you recover? +
We recover all types of assets including: unclaimed bank accounts, dividends, bonds, stocks, property deposits, pension funds, inheritance, insurance claims, and financial settlements. If it's rightfully yours, we can help recover it.
Is my information kept confidential? +
Absolutely. All information you provide is kept strictly confidential and secure. We use industry-standard encryption and never share your details with third parties without your explicit consent. Your privacy is our top priority.
How much does the free assessment cost? +
The assessment is completely free - $0. There are no hidden fees, no charges, and no obligation to proceed. We provide a comprehensive evaluation of your case at no cost to you.
What happens after I submit my case? +
After you submit your case, a licensed investigator will review your information within 24 hours. You'll receive a detailed assessment and recovery plan. If you choose to proceed, we'll begin the investigation process immediately.
Do you work with cases outside the USA? +
Yes, we handle cases globally. While we're based in the United States, we have experience recovering assets from international sources including Europe, Asia, and other regions. Contact us to discuss your specific situation.
What if my case is unsuccessful? +
If we're unable to recover your assets, you pay nothing. Our "No Win, No Fee" guarantee means you only pay a percentage of successfully recovered funds. There are no upfront costs or fees if recovery is unsuccessful.
